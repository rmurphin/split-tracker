About this plan

Five home sessions for two people alternating sets, about 45 minutes or less. Four upper days that each focus on two muscle groups, plus one leg day, balanced by working sets to 80% upper and 20% lower, with a short core block every day.

  • Dumbbells 10–50 lb
  • Pull-up bar
  • Dip bar
  • Bench
  • Barbell + plates

45-minute session

Warm-up 7Lifting ~31Core 6
  • Alternate every set: your partner's set is your rest, about 60–90 s.
  • On the first lift of the day, take an extra 30 s between rounds.
  • Lighter lifter goes first on barbell lifts so you only add plates.
  • Core: do it side by side, not alternating. Take turns on the pull-up bar for hanging raises.
  • Core work isn't counted in the 80/20 split.

Progression

  • Hit the top of the rep range on every set, then go up 5 lb next session.
  • Dips and pull-ups: once you clear 12–15 clean reps, hold a DB between your feet.
  • Legs get a full week to recover, so push Wednesday hard. When the 50s get too light for legs, slow the lowering to 4 seconds, add a 2-second pause at the bottom, or do 1½ reps.
  • No rack: clean the bar to your hips for rows, RDLs and hip thrusts. Use a spotter on heavy bench.
  • Every 6th week, deload: same exercises, half the sets, lighter loads.
